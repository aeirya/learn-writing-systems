Շնորհավոր ծնունդդ։

Happy birthday! · Letter-by-letter: sh-n-o-r-h-a-v-o-r ts-n-u-n-d-d (Eastern Armenian; spoken pronunciation includes unwritten vowels). The ending դ is written twice. The two dots ։ are the Armenian full stop.

Շնորհավոր

ծնունդդ

Use the left-hand shape for the initial Շ; use the right-hand shape for all the other letters. Ու / ու represents one vowel with two characters.
