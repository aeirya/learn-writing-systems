Transliteration key & spelling tips

These are Eastern Armenian letter-by-letter conventions, not automatic phonetic spelling. Capitalize a Latin letter or digraph to get its uppercase Armenian form. At the start of a word, ye and vo give ե and ո. A period becomes the Armenian full stop (։).

Use ch', ts', t', p', k' for aspirated consonants. uh (or ë) writes ը; eh writes է; oh writes օ; ew writes և. u writes the two-character vowel ու; w writes traditional ւ. For ambiguous spellings, type or paste the exact Armenian letter. Spoken vowels may be unwritten, so check unfamiliar words against a dictionary.

For paired illustrations, use the left-hand shape for uppercase and the right-hand shape for lowercase. The extracted ւ sample shows only lowercase; uppercase Ւ has a printed reference. Click an image to see it at full size.

Traditional and reformed Armenian alphabet

40 reference entries covering the traditional alphabet and the modern reformed inventory, including Ւ/ւ, Ու/ու and Եւ/և. Eastern and Western Armenian differ in several consonant sounds; romanization hints favor Eastern Armenian. IPA values are broad. In traditional spelling ւ often represents /v/; in reformed spelling it survives as a component of ու.
